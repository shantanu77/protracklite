Personal work dashboard · Product proposal · 05 October 2026

Make the dashboard
the start of a better workday.

An employee should know what matters next, what needs help, and what needs to be recorded within the first minute.

Audience: every employeeBased on the current application sourcePrototype uses fictional data
The highest-value change: one actionable personal work queue.

Turn the daily landing page into a place to prioritise, do the next action and close the loop. Each summary should lead to a useful decision or a specific task.

Clarity

Know which outcome to finish first and why it is urgent. Fewer repeated task cards and competing sections.

Continuity

See blockers, leave conflicts and pending handoffs before they interrupt delivery.

Confidence

Keep effort and attendance records accurate, with clear sync and approval states.

These benefits are product hypotheses to validate with employees. They are not measured improvements or claims about current usage.

04 · Recommended information hierarchy

Work first, supporting context nearby

  1. Personal header: organisation-local date, brief greeting or work summary, “Log effort” and “Add task”. Keep creation available without promoting AI before ordinary task creation.
  2. Four clickable summaries: today's focus, actions needing attention, planned versus available weekly hours, and completed planned outcomes. Each opens the relevant queue or supporting section.
  3. Main work queue: roughly 70% of the desktop workspace. Task ID, title, project, status, deadline, estimated remaining effort and a short reason for inclusion.
  4. Action inbox: roughly 30%. Blockers, missing past-day effort and missing attendance; each has a resolution action and a clear state.
  5. Weekly plan: five-day strip under the queue, configurable to the organisation's working week. Distinguish plans, recorded effort, today, approved leave and non-working days.
  6. Personal attendance: a compact snapshot and a link to the full profile. Show location, first/last punch, freshness and whether checkout is still open.

On mobile, present header → summaries → action inbox → focus queue → week/attendance. The prototype uses queue → week → action rail for simplicity; the implementation should promote urgent unresolved actions above the long queue on small screens.

Design direction

Quiet neutral surfaces, a dark navigation rail, one main action accent, and status text. Task color selection and color filters remain removed. Warning color communicates a status and always has a text label.

Keep the landing page personal

Manager/admin tools stay in their existing destinations. Employees see their own work and authorised shared-task context. Avoid a company leaderboard, a single “productivity score”, or chart-heavy modules with no next action.

06 · Definitions that make the dashboard trustworthy

Data, calculations and source rules

Tasks and deadlines
  • Use one date/time snapshot in the organisation timezone. Date-only deadlines become overdue on the next local day.
  • Focus excludes archived and completed tasks; blocked tasks are surfaced separately. A missing deadline is “No deadline”, never silently overdue.
  • “This week” means assigned open tasks due during the configured week, plus explicitly planned tasks. “Worked on this week” means tasks with effort recorded in that week.
  • Weekly completion numerator is completed items from the saved weekly plan; denominator is that plan's item count. Unplanned completions are shown separately. Shared work submitted for acceptance is not “closed”.
  • Remaining effort must come from a maintained estimate. If only total estimate and logged hours exist, label the derived value “Estimated remaining” and make correction possible.
Capacity and effort
  • Available weekly hours: scheduled working hours on non-holiday days minus approved leave hours, capped to avoid subtracting the same time twice. Do not count pending leave as confirmed absence; show it separately.
  • Planned hours: this week's allocation, not lifetime task estimates or hours already worked in unrelated weeks. Unknown allocations must be flagged as incomplete planning data.
  • Available planning margin: available hours minus planned hours. Negative means overcommitted; do not silently clamp the displayed result to zero.
  • Effort gap: compare past working-day allocation with actual recorded effort. A gap is a prompt to review, not proof that hours should be added. Today/future-day effort is not missing.
  • Backend validates hours, dates, visibility, notes and the action's permission. UI constraints alone are not sufficient; current task time-log routes need an audit before extending the day-level past-date rule.
Attendance, leave and regularization
  • Attendance is a presence record; effort is a work log. Do not infer one from the other.
  • Missing attendance is a past working day with no punch, no confirmed approved leave, and a successful sufficiently fresh sync. Weekends, holidays, future dates and unknown/unavailable feeds are not absence.
  • Regularization is always Remote · Noida, with 10:00–19:00 defaults, and goes to the manager. Pending requests are not worked days; do not offer duplicate requests.
  • Approved local regularizations override imported terminal labels in ProTrack's mode, counts and CSV. Zoho's original terminal metadata can remain unchanged; show provenance in the detail view.
  • For past months, use the entire month; for the current month, use completed days up to the chosen cutoff, with today's open status separate. Missing attendance must not be labelled confirmed leave.
  • Approval status comes from a confirmed integration result. The prototype does not claim that manager leave approval inside the app already exists; show the currently supported Zoho review link until a supported approval API is implemented.
Response contract and integration boundaries

Prefer one personal dashboard response with: as_of, timezone, tasks, weekly_plan, capacity, action_items, attendance, permissions, and source_status. Use stable task/request identifiers, explicit nulls for unknown values and separate integration freshness.

Existing task/effort actions, weekly plans, leave calendars, the capacity service and attendance feeds can be reused. New work includes aggregation, personal preferences/focus pins, blocker contact/next action, acknowledgment states, and background attendance refresh. This is a proposed contract; no new production endpoints were created for the prototype.

Permission-check all reads and writes against organisation, signed-in employee and task access. Avoid sequential Zoho calls on every dashboard visit. Load task data first, use cached attendance, and refresh in the background with a bounded timeout. Define and display a freshness threshold rather than making old attendance look current.

07 · Interaction specification

Short flows with a clear finish

Start the day

Open dashboard→Read focus + urgent actions→Open the highest-value task→See context and next step

Record effort

Select task / past day→Hours + useful notes→Validate & save→Refresh actual effort and gap

Retain unsaved input on errors. Disable repeat submission while saving. Show an inline actionable error and preserve existing data if the write fails. A successful save uses the returned server value.

Resolve a blocker

Open task → review reason and responsible contact → request help explicitly or update the blocker → mark dependency resolved only after it actually is. Permission-check updates; explain if a shared task must await owner action.

Regularize a missing remote day

Open missing attendance → verify date, Remote · Noida and login/logout → send for manager approval → show pending status. Manager approval is a distinct authorised flow. A sync failure leaves the request retryable and never reports success from a generic transport message.

Complete work

Open confirmation → choose valid completion date if required → save → update the matching plan's completion summary. Effort is not invented by completion. “Undo” must use a supported reopen action and confirmed server response; otherwise offer the task detail rather than a cosmetic reversal.

08 · Responsive, accessible and resilient

Specify the less visible states too

StateExpected presentation
Nothing due today“You're clear today” with next planned work and an optional focus action. Show no fabricated priorities.
No tasks / new employeeWelcome, a create/request-work action and a short explanation of how assignments appear.
No matching resultsShow the active filters, a clear-filters action and a result count; preserve the underlying queue.
Attendance unavailable / staleShow “Unavailable” or last successful sync. Keep tasks usable. Do not create missing-day warnings from absent data.
Saving or sync failureIndicate the specific operation, prevent duplicate submits, retain input and offer retry. A manager approval and a Zoho write have separate statuses.
Long task names / many assignmentsWrap titles without losing IDs or controls. Use pagination or progressive loading, stable ordering and server-scoped search.
Permission restrictedHide unauthorised actions, explain read-only shared work and enforce the same rule in the API.

Accessibility target: WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, labelled search/inputs, semantic tab state, logical dialog focus and Escape closing. Provide text with every status color, an announced save/error state, and reduced-motion behaviour. Touch targets should be at least 44px where possible; no hover-only essential action. Test 390px, tablet and desktop widths at 200% zoom.

Performance target to validate: usable core tasks within 2 seconds at p75 on representative devices/network. Treat Zoho status as an independent panel. Avoid blocking the landing page on HR integration responses. Use query batching, response caching and progressive enrichment.

09 · Delivery sequence

Deliver value before adding more widgets

Release 1 · Focus & speed

One queue, clearer names, details, effort/completion actions, search, responsive density, a useful default and basic usage instrumentation.

Release 2 · Close the loop

Action inbox, leave conflicts, weekly capacity, cached personal attendance, freshness and remote regularization entry point.

Release 3 · Personalise

Saved focus choices, optional modules, blocker escalation and evidence-based suggestions if the first releases demonstrate value.

Rollout approach

  1. Review the prototype with 5–8 employees across roles and 2 managers. Ask them to find today's priority, record effort and request attendance correction without guidance.
  2. Resolve definitions and task permission edge cases. Map existing endpoints before estimating implementation effort.
  3. Use an organisation/employee feature flag. Start with a small opt-in pilot and preserve the existing dashboard as the fallback.
  4. Compare a baseline week with a pilot period while accounting for leave, working schedules and task complexity. Fix failure states before expanding.
  5. Expand gradually after task actions, attendance classification, mobile layout and support burden are stable. Keep rollout independent of new AI features.

No implementation duration is promised here: effort depends on the notification model, caching, permission cases and planning data quality.

11 · Release gate

Concrete acceptance criteria

  1. The initial screen shows the signed-in employee's current focus without configuring blocks; all counts match the selected task/plan scope.
  2. A task appears once per selected view; search and project filters combine predictably; date boundaries respect the organisation timezone.
  3. Overdue and blocked are distinguishable by text, not color alone. No task color control or color filter is reintroduced.
  4. Effort and completion use current task permissions, valid dates and server confirmation. Shared-task work submission is not mistaken for final acceptance.
  5. Leave and holidays reduce available capacity once; unknown estimates do not silently become zero. Today's unrecorded effort is not labelled a past-day gap.
  6. Missing attendance excludes confirmed leave and non-working days; unavailable feeds do not create an absence warning. Requests are remote-only Noida and pending status is explicit.
  7. An approved regularization such as Priya's 01 Sep record displays Remote · Noida, counts toward remote rather than office, and appears consistently in CSV; original Zoho metadata is not represented as changed.
  8. Actions are keyboard-accessible and readable on mobile; dialogs retain input on save errors and restore focus after closing.
  9. No cross-employee or cross-organisation data appears unless explicitly authorised; background sync failures leave task work usable.
  10. Pilot success measures and rollback ownership are agreed before broad release. The prototype alone is not production implementation approval.
12 · Preview & research notes

How to review the prototype

Open the interactive dashboard

Try the task tabs, project filter, search, density toggle and task details. Complete a sample task, resolve the sample access blocker, add a task, log Wednesday's effort with an 80-character note, and send the sample remote regularization request. Reset restores the initial data. Forms are demonstrations with no API requests, email delivery or production writes.

The prototype uses a fixed 08 October 2026 scenario so yesterday's effort, today's focus and tomorrow's leave can be reviewed together. Its 26/32h weekly plan and the initial three completions are illustrative baselines; queue and inbox interactions are functional. Newly created sample tasks do not recalculate the full weekly plan, and completion summaries are simplified. Real backend permissions, sync, persistence, approval, date validation and allocation recalculation belong to implementation.
