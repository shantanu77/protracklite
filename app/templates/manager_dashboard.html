How metrics work

Overdue tasks: non-archived, open team tasks due before today.

Workload: estimated remaining effort on overdue tasks, tasks due this week and tasks selected in this week’s plan. Future tasks outside that set are excluded.

Remaining capacity: working hours from today to Sunday, less approved leave and hours already logged today. Holidays and configured weekends are excluded. Missing leave snapshots show Unknown.

Project pulse: all non-archived tasks assigned to the selected team. Closed / total gives progress. Stalled tasks mean Blocked; overdue work means At risk. This does not predict deadlines.

Goals: actual weighted achievement in the current financial-year plan, with its existing workflow status.

Effort: recorded task hours through yesterday against elapsed working capacity. Incomplete records do not establish low productivity.

The overview uses saved data and does not refresh Zoho or send notifications. Open Capacity View to refresh the shared leave snapshot.
